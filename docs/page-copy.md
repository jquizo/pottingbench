## Return and refund policy

Settings → Policies → Return policy *(Shopify's label may be "Return and refund policy")*.

> **Potting Bench is a concept store, not a real business. No orders are fulfilled.** This policy shows how a real store would describe its returns.
>
> **Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law**
>
> Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.
>
> If something arrives damaged or faulty, contact us with your order number and a photo.
>
> **Change of mind**
>
> This is on top of your rights above, not instead of them.
>
> - Planters, potting mix and plant food: we accept change-of-mind returns within 30 days if the item is unused and in its original packaging. You pay the return postage.
> - Live plants: we don't accept change-of-mind returns, because we can't resell a plant once it has left us.

### Why it's worded this way

- **No "no refunds" and no time limit on faults.** The consumer guarantees can't be excluded or limited to a period, including on sale items. Only change-of-mind returns can be refused or limited ([WA Consumer Protection: "no refunds" signs](https://www.consumerprotection.wa.gov.au/consumer-protection/no-refunds-0)).
- **The ACL paragraph** is the standard wording businesses use for warranties against defects. *(Check it against the Competition and Consumer Regulations 2010 before relying on it: AI quoted it from memory.)*
- **"Guarantees" here are the legal consumer guarantees,** not a marketing promise, so they don't break the "no guarantees" copy rule.
- **"Contact us"** has no contact details, because the store has none. A real store would give an email address.
- This isn't legal advice. A real merchant should have their policies reviewed.
